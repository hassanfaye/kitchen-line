# Kitchen Line

A pocket business tool for **private chefs, pop-ups and market booths**. One file (`index.html`) that runs in any phone or laptop browser. No sign-up and no server needed.

## What it does
- **Events**: track every gig from inquiry → quoted → booked → done. Covers private dinners, pop-ups, market booths, catering and cooking classes.
- **Dish library**: enter ingredients per portion once, and each dish shows its cost per plate plus allergens.
- **Pricing**:
  - Private dinners and catering: auto price per head from your costs + markup, with deposit, profit, margin and food-cost %.
  - Pop-ups and booths: price per plate, expected takings and **break-even plates**.
- **Client quote**: a branded proposal you can print or save as a PDF, or copy as a WhatsApp/email message.
- **Shopping list**: built automatically from the menu × guests (+ waste buffer), with tick-offs.
- **Prep & packing lists**: packing templates for each event type (booth gear, pop-up kit, etc.).
- **Money**: yearly takings and profit, which event type earns the most, and who still owes you.
- **Brand tab**: business name, colour, contact details and booking terms. Rebrand any time; quotes update instantly.

## Use it
Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, root) and add it to your phone's home screen.

Data is saved on the device. Use **Brand → Download backup** regularly.
